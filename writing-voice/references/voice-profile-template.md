## Conventions

- **Spelling**: <British / American, with the words that come up most>
- **References in running text**: <"Figure 4", "Fig. 4", `Figure~\ref{}`>
- **Units**: <siunitx macros, or spelled out>
- **Voice**: <passive and "this paper presents"; "we" only for choices the authors made;
  never "I">
- **Connectors used**: <In particular, However, Therefore, Finally, In summary>
- **Typography**: <no em-dashes; `--` only in ranges>
- **Names**: <the project name, what it may and may not be attached to, the correct
  spelling of each piece of apparatus, any name that has been corrected before>

## Document shapes expected

- **Abstract**: <one paragraph, at most N words, motivation, method, results with
  numbers, one-sentence consequence>
- **Introduction**: <problem, why it is hard, what this paper does, structure paragraph>
- **Body section**: <opener sentence, method, results with numbers, one-sentence
  interpretation>
- **Conclusions**: <formal summary that stands alone, one paragraph per study>
